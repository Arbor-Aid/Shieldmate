## What ShieldMate Can Learn

- Keep human approval gates explicit.
- Make audit trails first-class.
- Separate draft AI assistance from final human-approved records.
- Treat sensitive client and partner data as protected by default.
- Document who approved important actions.
- Keep retrieval and summarization grounded in source documents.

## What ShieldMate Should Avoid

- Do not let AI-generated summaries become authoritative without review.
- Do not hide the difference between draft and approved records.
- Do not expose sensitive data in screenshots, prompts, docs, or logs.
- Do not allow account-side or external actions without human approval.
- Do not treat vendor claims as final without live research and source review.
