# Google Ads First Campaign Notes

Date: 2026-06-02
Status: draft for review, not uploaded

## Campaign

Campaign name:

`2Marines Website Traffic - Public Hub - Search - 2026Q2`

Primary goal: drive qualified search traffic to the 2Marines public hub, ShieldMate page, store route, and MarineCoin page.

Landing pages:

- `https://2marines.us`
- `https://2marines.us/shieldmate`
- `https://2marines.us/store`
- `https://2marines.us/marinecoin`

Canonical Shopify storefront:

- `https://shieldmateapp.myshopify.com/`

Use `https://2marines.us/store` as the paid-search store landing page so the visible ad domain stays on `2marines.us`. The store route should redirect to Shopify.

## Guardrails

- Do not upload this campaign until Joshua approves copy, landing pages, budget, geo targeting, conversion tracking, and billing.
- Do not claim nonprofit/legal status beyond approved public wording.
- Do not make investment, token, crypto, donation-tax, government benefit, emergency service, or medical claims.
- MarineCoin copy must stay store/support focused and must not imply cryptocurrency, profit, investment, or financial return.
- Use Search campaign only for this first pass.
- Start with manual review in Google Ads Editor before any upload or publish.
