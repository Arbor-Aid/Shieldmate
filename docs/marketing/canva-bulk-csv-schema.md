## Field Notes

- `brand`: use `2 Marines`, `ShieldMate`, or `Marine Coins`.
- `primary_url`: use final registry URLs from `frontend/web/src/config/marketingLinks.ts`; keep placeholders until account URLs are confirmed.
- `utm_*`: follow `docs/marketing/ads-tracking-plan.md`.
- `approval_status`: use `draft`, `review`, `approved`, or `scheduled`.
- Keep copy grounded in the mission: veteran support, partner coordination, ShieldMate workflows, and Marine Coins commerce.
