# Marinescoin Launch Staging

Last updated: 2026-06-03

## Naming Note

Use `Marine Coins` in customer-facing copy where possible. Existing technical names such as `marinecoin` may remain in routes, build modes, hosting targets, filenames, or environment keys when they are already part of the repo convention.

## Scope

This document separates Marine Coins launch staging from the 2 Marines public site and ShieldMate app lanes. It is not a deployment approval.

- `docs/release/marinecoin-deployment-checklist.md`
- `docs/marketing/marketing-links-registry.md`

## Staging Checklist

- Confirm Marine Coins copy is reviewed independently from ShieldMate app copy.
- Confirm Shopify or storefront URLs are approved public URLs.
- Confirm no Shopify admin URLs appear in content.
- Confirm public contact email is `info@2marines.us`.
- Confirm legal, privacy, terms, disclaimer, and contact pages are reachable.
- Confirm copy does not describe Marine Coins as a cryptocurrency, token, security, investment, yield product, or promise of profit.
- Confirm product claims are factual and supportable.
- Confirm social posts use the Marine Coins brand lane.
- Confirm any waitlist or form flow has owner approval.
- Confirm deployment checklist is reviewed separately before any deploy.

## Website Readiness

- Landing page reviewed.
- Product or store CTA reviewed.
- Privacy page reviewed.
- Terms page reviewed.
- Disclaimer page reviewed.
- Contact page reviewed.
- Mobile layout reviewed.
- Links reviewed.

## Marketing Readiness

- Marine Coins rows in the 15-day social calendar reviewed.
- Store destination approved.
- Product imagery approved.
- Claims reviewed.
- UTM tagging confirmed.
- Meta staging plan confirmed if posts will run on Facebook or Instagram.

## Deployment Boundary

Marine Coins deployment readiness must not be bundled with the main 2 Marines public site deploy. Any deploy requires a separate approval and should follow the Marine Coins deployment checklist.

## Blockers

- Final storefront launch status.
- Final product imagery.
- Final legal review.
- Final deployment approval.
