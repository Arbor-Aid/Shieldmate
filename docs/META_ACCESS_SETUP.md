# Meta Access Setup

Last updated: 2026-06-03

## Scope

This checklist covers adding Yolanda and team members to Meta Business assets and billing access. It does not grant approval to launch ads or change spend.

## Required Inputs

- Meta Business portfolio name or ID.
- Facebook Page name or ID.
- Instagram account handle.
- Ad account name or ID.
- Yolanda's account email.
- Team member names, emails, and required roles.
- Billing role needed for each person.

## Access Principles

- Use least-privilege access.
- Require two-factor authentication for business access.
- Grant finance access only to people who need billing visibility or payment management.
- Do not share passwords, recovery codes, tokens, API keys, or card details.
- Record who approved each access grant.

## People Access Checklist

- Add Yolanda as a person in Meta Business settings.
- Assign the correct Facebook Page permissions.
- Assign the correct Instagram account permissions.
- Assign the correct ad account permissions.
- Assign finance access only if approved.
- Repeat for each team member.
- Confirm each invite is accepted.
- Confirm inactive or duplicate users are removed or left without access.

## Billing Access Checklist

- Confirm the ad account is the correct launch ad account.
- Confirm payment method exists without exposing card details in docs.
- Confirm billing threshold and spend limit with the account owner.
- Grant finance analyst or finance editor only as approved.
- Confirm no campaign is published during access setup.

## Completion Evidence

- Screenshot or account-side note showing accepted access.
- Date added.
- Approver.
- Role granted.
- Any billing permission granted.
